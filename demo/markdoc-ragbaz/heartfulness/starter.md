# A little more time

*Letters from an imaginary garden.*

There is a kind of afternoon that asks very little of us. A chair by the window;
the last of the tea; a letter begun without knowing quite where it will end.
This is a place for such beginnings.

## What the garden keeps

The roses have no particular interest in our plans. They lean into the path,
catch at a sleeve, and make a small interruption of the journey. Perhaps a page
may do the same: offer a pause, without insisting upon an answer.

> Leave a little room for what has not yet found its name.

## A second rising

We try a little less ornament. Then a little more. We keep what gives the words
warmth and put aside what gets in their way. Nothing here need be final.

*Mix, knead, bake, taste. Repeat.*
