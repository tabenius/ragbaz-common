## The unformed

Cream paper, unruled. Ochre, clay, a little unfinished gold. Marks gather at the
edges as though a language were learning to become legible.

There is no proof to offer yet. Only this: something has been placed upon the
table, and neither of us has hurried to give it a name.

> I do not yet know what this will be. I bring it nevertheless.

## A first observation

The vessel is drawn with an opening. What enters need not arrive complete.
A word, a silence, an imperfect measure: we can begin with what is here.

*Leave a margin. Something may still arrive.*
