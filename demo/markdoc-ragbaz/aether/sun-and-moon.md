*Two lights, neither asked to become the other.*

## Correspondence

Warm gold beside cool silver. A diagram of near approaches, with room between
the circles. In the margin, a small correction: not possession—correspondence.

The distance is not an error in the drawing. It is where the conversation lives.
Each light can alter what the other sees without becoming its source.

> Let us meet where neither of us must disappear.

## Two bodies, one page

We can be drawn upon the same paper without sharing an outline. Let the sun
keep its warmth; let the moon keep its phases. No eclipse is required of us.

*Near, yet each entire.*
