## An unfinished field

Soot, plum-black, iron ink. A narrow copper line passes through the dark and
stops before reaching the border. Nothing explains it. Someone has left space
beneath it to continue.

This is not a promise that every darkness conceals a revelation. Some nights
are simply nights. The page asks for no conclusion before its time.

> I cannot see the shape of this yet. I am still here, writing.

## A place left open

The orbit has a break in it. Not a secret entrance, not an answer: a place where
the hand lifted from the paper. We may return. We may begin another line.

*Not yet is not the same as nothing.*
