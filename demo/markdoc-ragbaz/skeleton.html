    Reading modes & keyboard shortcuts

    Flow reads vertically. Left/right enters paper view: two pages per desktop screen, one on smaller screens. Up/down or j/k returns to flow at the current passage. The first horizontal key changes mode; subsequent presses turn spreads.

    Lowercase l is reserved for the legend; Shift+L is next spread. Shortcuts do not intercept typing, menus, source inspection or modified browser commands.
