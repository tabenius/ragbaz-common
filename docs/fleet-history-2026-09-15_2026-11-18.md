# Fleet history, 2026-09-15 → 2026-11-18

What the RAGBAZ fleet did over these nine weeks. Reconstructed by a Claude
session on 2026-11-18 from git history only: 1,290 non-merge commits on all
branches of 28 repositories. Some repositories were not examined (see
*Coverage*). This is an **observation of commits, not of intent**. Sessions
were not read. Most commits carry no agent trailer, so authorship by harness
is mostly unknown (see *Who*).

## Rhythm and interruptions

Commits per day show the breaks the owner described: budget, hardware,
office moves.

| Period | Activity | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 15 – Oct 3 | steady, 20–56/day | Normal multi-repo work |
| Oct 4 – Oct 15 | moderate, 6–42/day | Normal, with smaller slices |
| **Oct 16 – Oct 29** | **sparse: 0–19/day, eight days ≤1** | First interruption. WIP "checkpoint … model handoff" commits on Oct 20 in frog and ragbaz-dash |
| Oct 30 – Nov 2 | **burst: 170, 24, 205, 91** | Catch-up: large merges in ragbaz-common, ragbaz-dash, rebekah, weftmark |
| **Nov 3 – Nov 12** | **almost silent (1 commit on Nov 3, 15 on Nov 7)** | Second, longer interruption |
| Nov 13 – Nov 18 | recovering, 16–82/day | New projects (BAZ.NG-5), releases, rebsync/rbq, mneme |

The handoff machinery was built between the two interruptions, Oct 20–Nov 2,
which fits the owner's account.

## What happened, by week

- **W38 (Sep 14–20).** rebekah bootstrapped as a Nix-built image. v-BAZ boots:
  Secure Boot, ZFS, offline first boot, Wi-Fi. BAZ.AI-Governance gets an Ephor
  governance Worker. weftmark gets a governance evidence kind and forge-adapter
  hardening.
- **W39 (Sep 21–27).** Human-in-the-loop: the Ephor MCP gate holds agent tool
  calls until a person decides from Dash. nostoi becomes the tamper-evident
  audit-chain library with CLI and TUI. ragbaz-dash adds connector keys and
  dialogdoc. www.ragbaz adds passkeys and email sign-in. CI moves to GitHub
  cloud runners while ragbaz-deploy is offline.
- **W40 (Sep 28–Oct 4).** nostoi gains anchors, S3 fan-out, revocation and
  kernel-log ingestion. The design-system's terminal tokens reach frog and
  ragbaz-operator-tui, "the fleet's terminal entry point". weftmark, sylvae
  and frog record intents in nostoi.
- **W41 (Oct 5–11).** ragbaz-common: Fragment 0.1 and the **cooperation
  protocol**. The Daily Agenda arrives in ragbaz-daily-agenda, Dash and rebekah.
  remote-android gets its Rust sidecar and reliability work. indra and
  ragbaz-bridge-android add authenticated debug peers. kali declares its host
  SBOM. CI gets concurrency groups fleet-wide.
- **W42 (Oct 12–18).** frog gets a portable Rust model, live web kanban and a
  D1 pilot. rebekah exposes change-requests and Frog task snapshots. nostoi
  adds sealed fields and audited erasure. remote-android writes its
  `.handoff/` docs. www.ragbaz lists the fleet projects publicly.
- **W43 (Oct 19–25).** *First interruption.* frog does a legacy-migration
  inventory. kali adds Alertmanager and disk/inode warnings. **mneme is born**:
  session index, collector, MCP tools, semantic retrieval. Dash gets personal
  read-only MCP access and a WIP "model handoff" checkpoint.
- **W44 (Oct 26–Nov 1).** Oct 26: Dash's **Hypomnema sink and personal
  handoff MCP**, and `mneme evac`. Then the catch-up burst. ragbaz-common: evidence inspector,
  Replicad bundler, Chart.js/Cytoscape adapters. ragbaz-dash: Pieces Labs,
  offline recovery PWA. rebekah: workspace-handoff/OCI contract and
  interoperability CI. weftmark: PyInstaller binaries and .deb. mneme: evac
  and portable builds. token-economy: pricing foundation and Codex Online
  handoff.
- **W45 (Nov 2–8).** Nov 2 is the last busy day before the *second
  interruption* (Nov 3–12). What landed is release infrastructure: signed release candidates registered for RAGBAZ R2 in nostoi,
  sylvae, rebekah, v-BAZ and weftmark, and www.ragbaz's signed import and
  promotion. frog: recoverable migrations.
- **W46 (Nov 9–15).** Work resumes Nov 13. BAZ.NG-5 (Mímisbrunr noise machine) is born and
  featured. Every project gets `ragbaz.component.json` schema 0.2.0.
  detcordon aligns its toolchain and pins bpf-linker. rebsync gains Mneme,
  Frog and agenda layers, phone sync, `serve` and the APK workflow. mneme
  adds branch-aware navigation and erasure of derived models.
- **W47 (Nov 16–18).** rebsync becomes **Rebeqah / `rbq`**: CLI tarballs, peer
  transports, release script. mneme adds retention and snapshot contract
  0.2.1. weftmark makes its first stable release, 0.1.0. nostoi gets a Debian
  package and signed R2 mirror. www.ragbaz renames the product to Rebeqah;
  the rebsync allowlist sits on branch `feat/rebeqah-release-signing`,
  **unmerged**. The GitHub Actions free tier is exhausted for the first time.

## Who

sessions), `~/.codex/sessions` and `~/.claude/projects` on the hosts to
attribute them.
